An interactive, animated "bar chart race" tracing the evolution of arms transfers by China and the United States from 1950 to 2024. The animation shows how each supplier's leading partners shift over time, offering an accessible way to explore long-run patterns in the global arms trade.

The project complements my research on China's arms transfers (see "Armed Dragon! An Empirical Analysis of China's Arms Transfers").

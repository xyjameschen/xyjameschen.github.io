My recent research examines the policy preferences of Chinese citizens in domestic governance and foreign relations. I have used survey experiments, public-opinion data, country-year political-economy data, and text-as-data to examine questions in empirical social science. Substantive topics include China's arms transfers, Chinese public attitudes toward foreign and cross-Strait relations, and trends in social contention in China.

I am a Ph.D. student in political science at National Taiwan University, and I earned an M.A. in Contemporary China Studies from National Tsing Hua University.
